## style_1.jpg — The Starry Night (1889)
**Artist:** Vincent van Gogh  
**Source:** Museum of Modern Art (MoMA), New York  
**License:** Public Domain — work created before 1928, no copyright restrictions  
**Why chosen:** Swirling brushwork and high-contrast color create dramatic, immediately recognizable style transfer results against any background.

## style_2.jpg — The Great Wave off Kanagawa (c. 1831)
**Artist:** Katsushika Hokusai  
**Source:** The Metropolitan Museum of Art Open Access Collection  
**License:** Public Domain (CC0) — The Met Open Access Policy  
**Why chosen:** Strong geometric wave patterns and flat ukiyo-e color areas test NST on a non-Western, non-painterly visual language.

**Artist:** Wassily Kandinsky  
**Source:** Solomon R. Guggenheim Museum, New York
